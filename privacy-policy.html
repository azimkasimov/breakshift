Breakshift – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

In short: Breakshift has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. To improve the game we collect anonymous gameplay statistics (for example which levels are hard) and crash reports with Google Firebase; they are not linked to your name, email or advertising ID. Your progress is saved on your device only. Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store.

Who we are

Breakshift is a game made by FunnyBall Games ("we"). You can contact us through the support page.

What we do not collect

  • names, email addresses, phone numbers or other contact details;
  • location, photos, contacts, microphone or camera data;
  • advertising identifiers: the game shows no ads and does no tracking (advertising ID collection is switched off);
  • messages: the game has no chat and no free text.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the game crashes or hits an error, Google Firebase Crashlytics sends us a crash report: what went wrong in the code, the device model, operating system and app version, and a random installation ID. It contains no personal information and is used only to fix bugs. Crash reports are kept for 90 days.

Gameplay statistics (Firebase Analytics)

The game uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how it is played, so we can fix difficulty and bugs. It records events such as a level started or finished (level number, turns, stars), a Siege run started or ended, a star chest opened, the unlock screen shown and whether a purchase succeeded, together with basic technical data (device model, operating system and app version, country derived from the connection, a random app-instance ID, and session times). It does not collect your name, email, contacts, precise location or advertising ID, and the data is not used for ads or personalisation and is not sold. Google processes it on our behalf under the Firebase privacy terms; we see only totals and trends. Analytics data is kept for at most 14 months and then deleted.

Information stored on your device

The game saves a small file on your device: levels completed, stars, Salvage and unlocks, run progress, and settings such as sound. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it. We have no copy and cannot restore it for you.

Purchases

Breakshift is free to download. Its optional in-app purchases are the Full Game Unlock and two cosmetic ball-look packs, each bought once. The purchase is processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own privacy policies. We never see your payment details or your account. To recognise your purchase, the game uses Unity In-App Purchasing together with the core of Unity Gaming Services. They are used only to process purchases and to restore them on request. We do not use them for advertising or profiling. As with most online services, a connection to a Unity service may expose technical information such as your IP address to that provider while a purchase is being processed; we do not receive or store it.

Children

Breakshift contains no ads, no chat and no social features. It is not directed at children under 13. The anonymous gameplay statistics described above never include advertising IDs or contact details.

Deleting your data

We hold no data that identifies you. To remove everything the game stored on your device, uninstall it. The anonymous statistics are deleted automatically within 14 months. Purchase records are kept by Google or Apple; manage them in your store account.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date above.

Contact
