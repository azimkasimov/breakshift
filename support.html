Breakshift – Help

Need help? Email write2kasimov@gmail.com. Tell us your phone model, and open Settings in the game to find the version number. We usually reply within two working days.

I bought something but it did not unlock

Close the game completely and open it again: the store hands over any purchase that did not finish. Then open Settings → Restore Purchase. Use the same Google Play or Apple account that made the purchase. If it is still locked, email us with the order number from your receipt.

What is free and what is in the Full Game Unlock?

Free: PLAY levels 1-30, Siege Act 1, the Daily Wall, Demolition and the Workshop. The one-time Full Game Unlock adds Siege Acts 2-3 and all PLAY levels after 30. Two optional ball-look packs (Neon and Cosmic) change only how the balls look, never how they play. Everything is bought once and kept; there are no ads and no subscriptions.

I have a new phone. Can I keep my progress?

Progress is stored on the device only, and there is no account or cloud backup. A new phone starts fresh, but your purchase comes back with Settings → Restore Purchase.

Do I need internet to play?

No. Breakshift works fully offline. You only need a connection to buy or restore purchases.

A shot looked unfair

Breakshift is built to be fair and repeatable: the same shot always gives the same result. If you think something is wrong, email us with the mode, the level or act, and what happened.

Privacy and deleting data

We collect no personal data, only anonymous gameplay statistics (Firebase Analytics) to improve the game. Uninstalling the game removes everything stored on the phone. See the privacy policy.
